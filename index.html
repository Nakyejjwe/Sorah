FAQ

Questions before you order

How long does delivery take?

Orders close every Friday at 8 PM. Your items usually reach Kampala 2–3 weeks later, and we deliver within a day or two of arrival.

How do I pay?

Pay a 50% deposit by MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money to confirm your pre-order. You pay the balance and delivery fee when your order arrives.

Can you buy from a store that's not on this site?

Yes. Send the product link with your size and colour. We reply with the full delivered price, and only buy once you approve it.

What if it doesn't fit?

Overseas stores don't accept returns from Uganda, so send your measurements before ordering. We'll check the store's size chart with you.

Is there anything you can't bring in?

We can't ship power banks, lithium batteries, flammable items or liquids over 100ml. Ask us if you're unsure about an item.

How do I track my order?

Every order gets a reference like SOR-AB12C. Tap Track order and enter it, or send it to us on WhatsApp.

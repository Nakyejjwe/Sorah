— About Sorah

Two worlds.
One vision.

Sorah is a Kampala-based creative and technology brand built by a woman who refused to choose between style and substance. On one side, a women's fashion preorder business that delivers curated, quality pieces with care and intentionality. On the other, an IT consultancy — specialising in digital security, data analytics, and systems that actually work for real people.

Both sides of Sorah share the same DNA: precision, purpose, and the belief that excellence is non-negotiable — whether in what you wear or how you protect your data.
